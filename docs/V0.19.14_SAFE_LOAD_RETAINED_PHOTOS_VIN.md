# v0.19.14 — Retained arrival photos and local neural VIN reading

## What changed

### One onsite-photo section, before and after Submit

The phone no longer clears accepted front/back/VIN files after QA or a release update. The same onsite section remains visible after confirmation as read-only evidence. Capture/Retake controls disappear when released or cancelled; the photos do not disappear with them.

When the operator reopens a load, the app retrieves its current-assignment saved attempt metadata and loads the protected front, optional back and VIN images with authorization headers. Tokens are not put in image URLs. Local captures take preview priority while correcting evidence. Saved images loaded after reopening are read-only and are never silently resubmitted. To create a new attempt after reopening, capture front and VIN photos again, with the optional back when needed.

Changing loads/assignment revisions or signing out still clears private local drafts. Saved server evidence stays encrypted and available to authorized reviewers. Previous attempts/assignments remain in broker history and custody rather than being displayed as the current assignment's photos. The app ignores late upload/progress results after a server/login change.

### New local neural VIN reader

Barcode reading remains first. When no VIN barcode is readable, a CPU-based RapidOCR/ONNX reader detects and recognizes text lines, with contrast and orientation fallbacks. Legacy Tesseract remains a fallback when no complete high-score neural result is available. Only complete 17-character VIN candidates are considered; different candidates hold the load. The extracted VIN must still exactly match the dispatcher assignment. The reader receives only the image, not the expected VIN. It does not fill missing characters or apply character substitutions based on the expected value.

The new reader successfully extracted all 17 characters from the supplied dirty-window VIN screenshot, matching an independent manual transcription. The screenshot is not included in the project. This is evidence of improvement on that example, not a promise that every dirty, blurred, reflective or partially hidden VIN will read correctly. OCR scores are text-recognition scores, not identity/authenticity confidence.

The actual reader used is saved in each attempt's extraction metadata (`vin_reader`: local barcode, neural OCR, Tesseract, or legacy onsite scan). Confirmation receipts, broker notifications/callback, prior-rejection holds, exact comparison and custody behavior remain. An already rejected load still needs broker authorization; a new good photo does not erase its rejection history.

## Dependencies and privacy

No OCR API key, external photo upload or GPU is required. The pinned RapidOCR wheel includes the models, which run locally on the backend CPU; no model download occurs during photo QA. ONNX telemetry is disabled. Normal Docker image building needs access to download Python dependencies. The backend uses pinned ONNX Runtime and OpenCV builds plus their Linux runtime libraries. Existing migrations and PostgreSQL/document volumes are unchanged.

RapidOCR dependency/model provenance: https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR and https://pypi.org/project/rapidocr-onnxruntime/1.4.4/ . Dependency licenses are distributed with the installed packages. This is text extraction, not facial identification, DMV authentication or proof of truck ownership.

## Update both the PC and phone

Preserve your .env and Docker volumes. Copy the ZIP's inner ValhallaFreight contents over C:\GitHub\ValhallaFreight, including backend/requirements.txt, backend/Dockerfile and safe-load-mobile. This is a complete source ZIP, not a backup of your live database or uploaded identity photos.

Stop the OLD Metro window with Ctrl+C. Then:

```powershell
Set-Location C:\GitHub\ValhallaFreight
docker compose -p valhallafreight up -d --build
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\safe-load-mobile\Start-SafeLoad.ps1 -ComputerIP 192.168.1.195
```

The backend build will install the new local reader and models. Scan the NEW QR in Expo Go SDK58 and verify **App v0.19.14**. Server URL: http://192.168.1.195:8000 (adjust if your PC address changed).
