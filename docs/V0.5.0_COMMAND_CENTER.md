# Valhalla Freight v0.5.0 — Functional Command Center

The Command Center now uses live database-derived metrics rather than visual fallback values.

## KPI definitions
- **Active shipments**: shipment records whose current status is not Delivered or Cancelled.
- **Revenue this month**: sum of `customer_charge` for shipments created in the current calendar month.
- **Gross profit**: customer charge minus `final_carrier_cost` when available, otherwise minus expected `carrier_cost`.
- **Delivered on time**: deliveries with both `delivered_at` and `estimated_delivery`; on time means delivered on or before ETA.
- Additional optional KPIs: open quotes, carrier cost, blended margin.

KPI selection is browser-local in v0.5.0. Organization-level dashboard configuration is a future persistence step.

## Network Performance
Real shipment financials are available for 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 12 months, or a custom start/end date. Bucketing automatically changes between daily, weekly, and monthly based on the selected range.

## Valhalla Freight Intelligence
- Margin exceptions are derived from shipments where final carrier cost exceeds expected carrier cost by more than $75.
- Quote opportunity savings are derived from the spread between the highest and lowest normalized customer prices on multi-carrier quotes.

Both intelligence metrics open drill-down drawers.

## Recent Shipments
Recent shipment rows are backed by the shipment database and open a shipment detail workspace.

## Import Freight
CSV/XLSX/XLS import is now functional. Customers are matched by customer code or customer name and carriers by SCAC. Imported shipment financials contribute to Command Center metrics.
