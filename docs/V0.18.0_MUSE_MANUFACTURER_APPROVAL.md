# v0.18.0 — Manufacturer opt-in, original credit application, and Muse booking

## Set up access

1. Sign in as **Valhalla Freight Admin**.
2. Open **Customers → manufacturer → Commercial → Muse Freight access**. Check **Offer Muse Freight rates to this manufacturer**, then save. New and existing manufacturers default to off. Access is shared by the manufacturer and the customers it owns.
3. In **Users & Company Access**, create a login with the new **Muse Freight Admin** role and assign its company to your Muse Freight company record (create that company first if needed). There is no pre-created Muse login or new default password.
4. Muse Freight Admin signs in through the normal sign-in page and lands in its separate **Muse Partner Portal**. Super Admin can also open it through **Administration → Muse Portal**. Manufacturer users cannot access Muse administration. Muse admins cannot access Valhalla account management, other carrier accounts, or non-opted-in manufacturers' applications.

## Manufacturer application

- Muse rates show only while the manufacturer is opted in.
- Clicking **Select** on a Muse rate opens the application prompt until approval. The original blank PDF is bundled byte-for-byte from https://cdntms.taicloud.net/tms/Credit_Application.pdf. The form shown inside the software uses a rendered copy of that exact template; entries occupy its existing blanks. The wording, headings, privacy notice, and layout are unchanged.
- The authorized applicant types the signature and print name, enters the date as YYYY-MM-DD, and confirms authorization before submitting. This records an electronic submission; it does not independently verify the signer's identity.
- Submitted application values are encrypted at rest using the existing backend encryption configuration. Keep INTEGRATION_SECRET_KEY (or the existing JWT_SECRET_KEY fallback) unchanged across upgrades so applications remain readable.
- An application is immutable after submission. Declining it requires a reason, and the manufacturer can submit a new application while the prior version remains in the review history.
- Muse sees pending applications and a count in its portal. A notification is also saved, and email is queued for active Muse Freight Admin logins. Without SMTP configuration, email is captured in the local outbox instead of being delivered. Banking details are not placed in notifications, emails, or activity logs.
- The completed PDF is available in the software to authorized manufacturer users, Muse admins, and Super Admin. Filled documents are not publicly hosted.

## Muse review and quoting

In **Muse Partner Portal → Manufacturers**, open a manufacturer and review its completed application. **Approve booking access** enables Muse bookings for that manufacturer's authorized users and owned customer accounts. The old Super Admin “mark verified” shortcut no longer grants booking access.

In **Muse quotes**, Muse can enter the actual carrier, customer price, carrier buy cost, and transit days for a manufacturer quote. A mapped carrier, cost, and transit are required before an approved manufacturer can select and book. Super Admin can also maintain these on the saved quote. Existing company carrier options remain separate even when the same carrier has a Muse rate.

## Bookings and BOLs

After approval, the manufacturer selects the Muse offer, confirms the real shipping facilities, and creates the shipment and its BOL through the existing shipment confirmation workflow. The request appears under **Muse Partner Portal → Shipment bookings** and is marked **pending Muse confirmation** in the manufacturer's shipment record.

Muse uses **View BOL** to inspect/print the shipment, arranges the carrier booking, then confirms the carrier booking reference and PRO in the portal. The manufacturer then sees the confirmed PRO. Retries do not send a Muse shipment to a manufacturer's direct carrier API. Duplicate booking, stale offers, expired quotes, disabled manufacturers, and unapproved applications are blocked server-side.

**Live Muse rate/dispatch APIs are not connected.** Actual prices are entered by Muse or Super Admin. The in-software booking request and Muse confirmation workflow work locally; carrier dispatch still needs Muse's real operational confirmation. No fake carrier confirmations are generated.

## Upgrade locally

Replace source in your existing Compose project directory; retain your .env, project name, and PostgreSQL/document volumes. Run:

```sh
docker compose up -d --build
docker compose ps
```

Open http://localhost:5173. Startup adds the new Muse tables and nullable carrier/cost/transit columns to existing Muse quotes. Existing migrations, PostgreSQL rows, and volume definitions are preserved.
