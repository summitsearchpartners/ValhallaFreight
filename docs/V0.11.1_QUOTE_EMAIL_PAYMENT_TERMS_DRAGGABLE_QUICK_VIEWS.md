# Valhalla Freight v0.11.1

## Updates

- Shipment Quick View opens centered and can be dragged by its header in Finance Center, Freight Planner and Shipment Visibility.
- Freight Planner calendar entries show the assigned carrier and SCAC when available.
- Quote Studio markup controls are compact and positioned immediately below the Ready to Rate / Carrier Options heading.
- Markup remains an internal pricing control; customer quote emails show only the final sell price.
- A selected carrier rate can be emailed to the customer directly from Quote Studio. SMTP delivery uses the existing Valhalla email service and safely captures messages in local development when SMTP is not configured.
- Quote Studio now captures freight payment terms: Prepaid (default), Collect, Third Party, Third Party Prepaid, or Third Party Collect.
- Payment terms persist on quotes and shipments and flow into carrier API mapping and the generated BOL.
