# Valhalla Freight v0.9.1 — Shipment Confirmation References & BOL Contacts

This release moves shipment-specific references and facility contact details earlier into the quote approval workflow.

## Shipment confirmation
- PO number
- Customer reference
- Ship From contact name, phone, email, and pickup instructions
- Ship To/consignee contact name, phone, email, and delivery instructions
- Saved Customer 360 locations automatically prefill stored contact and instruction values.

## Shipment persistence
PO number and customer reference are saved on the Shipment record at creation. Facility contact and instruction details remain embedded in the normalized origin/destination address snapshots so the shipment retains the exact operational information used when it was booked.

## Bill of Lading
The professional Valhalla BOL now prints shipper and consignee contact information directly in the corresponding address blocks, including pickup/delivery instructions when supplied. Quoted carrier cost has been removed from the BOL because brokerage buy cost is internal financial information.
