# Valhalla Freight v0.7.0 — Carrier 360 & Carrier Network

## Purpose
Carrier 360 becomes the network-side equivalent of Customer 360: one authoritative carrier record for qualification, service capability, commercial strategy, operations, finance, performance, claims, and future provider integrations.

## Carrier master
- Carrier name, legal name, SCAC, MC, DOT
- Primary mode and active state
- Preferred carrier and routing rank
- Authority, onboarding, safety rating
- API / EDI connectivity state
- Website, operating address, dispatch, billing, phone
- Payment terms, factoring, cargo and liability limits

## Qualification & compliance
Carrier compliance records support authority, cargo insurance, auto liability, W-9, carrier agreements, safety reviews, hazmat certification, and other requirements. Each record has status, expiration, verification date, and optional document reference.

## Service network
- Supported modes and service levels
- Equipment / freight-type capability
- Geographic service areas
- Terminals and service centers
- Strategic lanes with target carrier cost and average transit

## Commercial records
- Carrier contracts and references
- Carrier document vault metadata
- Carrier-linked Pricing Engine rules
- Target lane cost strategy

## Performance
Performance is derived from actual Valhalla shipment records wherever data exists:
- Shipment count
- On-time pickup: actual pickup <= scheduled pickup
- On-time delivery: actual delivery <= customer required delivery (fallback to carrier ETA)
- Average observed transit
- Carrier spend and average shipment cost
- Customer revenue, brokerage GP and margin
- Claims count, amount and claims ratio
- Observed lane rollups

## Activity history
Carrier changes and notes are recorded as Carrier Activity records so integrations and manual operations can share one future audit timeline.

## Integration boundary
RMIS, Highway, MyCarrierPortal, SaferWatch, DAT, Truckstop and carrier-direct connectivity should synchronize *into* the Valhalla carrier model. Provider records should never replace the internal carrier master.

## Architecture
Backend: `backend/app/domains/carriers/`

Frontend: `frontend/src/features/carriers/`

Carrier child records use separate tables and endpoints so future changes to compliance, lanes, contacts or documents do not require expanding one giant carrier table or UI file.
