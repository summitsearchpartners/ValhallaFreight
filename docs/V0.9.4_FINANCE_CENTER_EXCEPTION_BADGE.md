# Valhalla Freight v0.9.4 — Finance Center Exception Badge

The Finance Center navigation item now shows a live badge whenever unresolved carrier invoice variances have crossed a customer margin-protection threshold.

- Badge count is derived from Finance Center audit rows with status `exception`.
- The count refreshes every 30 seconds along with the header notification feed.
- Resolving an exception by approving or disputing the carrier bill removes it from the badge on the next refresh.
- The badge remains visible in both expanded and collapsed sidebar modes.
- Reading the notification bell does not clear the Finance Center badge because the badge represents unresolved finance work, not unread messages.
