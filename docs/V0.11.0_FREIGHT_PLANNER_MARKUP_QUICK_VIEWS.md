# Valhalla Freight v0.11.0

## Quote Studio markup controls
Quote Studio now supports a per-quote pricing override in the Ready to Rate panel. Flat markup adds a dollar amount to carrier cost. Margin sets a target gross margin percentage and calculates the customer sell rate from carrier cost. Existing customer/pricing-engine rules remain the fallback when no explicit override is supplied.

## Freight Planner
A new Freight Planner workspace provides a month calendar for inbound and outbound shipments across customer locations. Sites are color-coded consistently within the current view, a customer filter is available, and calendar shipment events open a shipment quick-view modal.

## Finance Center shipment quick view
Shipment numbers in carrier-bill audit rows are clickable and open the related shipment in an in-page modal, preserving Finance Center context.

## Shipment Visibility quick view
Shipment rows in Visibility Center now open the same shipment quick-view modal instead of navigating away from visibility operations.
