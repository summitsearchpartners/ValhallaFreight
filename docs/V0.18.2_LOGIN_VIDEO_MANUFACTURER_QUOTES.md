# v0.18.2 — Cinematic login and manufacturer quoting

## Login

The left login panel now uses a locally bundled, muted, looping highway freight film with a dark cinematic overlay, larger typography, and compact glass feature cards. The Valhalla branding and Muse partner mark remain.

- Pause/resume is available on the video panel.
- The video pauses when the tab is hidden.
- Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences use the poster instead of autoplay.
- The compact/mobile login keeps the sign-in form without downloading a hidden video.
- If video loading fails, the poster remains and sign-in stays usable.
- WebM and MP4 encodings plus the poster are included in the project, so the background does not depend on another website remaining online.

Footage credit and source information are in [LOGIN_MEDIA_CREDITS.md](LOGIN_MEDIA_CREDITS.md). Muse's website was the visual reference; the bundled footage is a separate stock clip.

## Manufacturer Quote Studio

Manufacturer and Manufacturer Admin accounts no longer see a Customer selector. Quote Studio automatically loads and uses the company assigned to the login. It works even when the manufacturer has no child customer records.

Broker and Super Admin customer selection remains available. Existing quotes, company data, and historical customer assignments are not rewritten. Muse access and approval rules continue to apply. No backend permissions or database schema changes are part of this release.

## Local testing

Keep your existing project directory, Compose project name, .env, and PostgreSQL/document volumes. Replace the application source and run:

```sh
docker compose up -d --build
```

Open http://localhost:5173 and sign out to view the new login screen. Sign in as a manufacturer, enter the lane and freight details directly, and compare rates.

Checks cover the frontend build, backend regression suite, video playback and pause/resume, reduced-motion and failed-video fallbacks, mobile login width, manufacturer quoting without child customers, retained broker selection, and the existing Muse application/approval/shipment/BOL walkthrough.
