# Valhalla Freight v0.8.7 — Quote Record Usability & Simplified Accessorials

## Quote Record layout
The quote review workspace now prioritizes shipment confirmation after a rate is selected. Rated Lane, quoted accessorials, and Carrier Options remain grouped in the left review column while Shipment Confirmation receives the larger right-side workspace.

The duplicate selected-carrier summary was removed from Shipment Confirmation because the selected carrier is already highlighted in Carrier Options.

On wide screens Ship From and Ship To confirmation are displayed side-by-side. The layout collapses responsively on smaller displays.

## Simplified accessorials
Location-specific delivery selections that normally resolve to limited-access handling have been consolidated into **Limited Access Delivery**. This includes historical selections for schools/universities/libraries, military/correctional/nuclear sites, hotels/restaurants, churches/nursing homes/country clubs, carnivals/fairs, camps/marinas, construction sites, farms/ranches, quarries/mines, self-storage, utilities/plants, and mills.

Airport, appointment/call-before, inside delivery, liftgate, residential, and trade-show services remain distinct because they commonly map to separate carrier accessorial codes and pricing.

Legacy quote records remain compatible: older detailed accessorial codes are normalized to the new consolidated selection when displayed or confirmed.
