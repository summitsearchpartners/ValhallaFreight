# Valhalla Freight v0.11.5

## Centered, draggable Email Selected Quote modal

- Email Selected Quote now opens centered in the viewport.
- The modal can be moved by dragging its header.
- Clicking the shaded backdrop closes the modal.
- The close button, email input, Cancel, and Send Quote controls remain interactive and do not initiate dragging.
- The window position resets to center each time it is opened.
- Existing quote email privacy behavior remains unchanged: carrier buy cost, markup, and gross profit are not exposed to the customer.
