# Valhalla Freight v0.10.1 – Booking Flow Cleanup & Smart Location Review

## Booking workflow
The shipment confirmation form in Quote Bucket is the final operational data-entry step before booking. Approving the shipment creates the Valhalla shipment/BOL and immediately invokes the selected carrier adapter. The Shipment Control Tower opens directly afterward; the former automatic Complete Shipment Details modal no longer reopens.

## New facility intelligence
Google-verified one-off Ship From and Ship To facilities continue to be promoted into Customer 360. Valhalla seeds Appointment, Liftgate, Residential and Limited Access defaults from explicitly selected accessorials and from clear instruction keywords.

Because absence of a keyword cannot prove a service is not required, every newly saved facility also generates an internal review notification. The notification links to Customer 360 Locations, where the location can now be edited and its defaults confirmed.

## Notifications
The global notification bell now merges freight-audit/margin-protection alerts with operational alerts such as new-location review. Finance-specific customer portal notifications remain on the existing finance endpoint.
