# Valhalla Freight v0.7.2 — LTL Quote Workflow

This release separates **rating data** from **shipment execution data**.

## Rating
LTL Quote Studio only requires customer, origin ZIP, destination ZIP, freight details, special services/accessorials, and an optional required-delivery date. This keeps rating fast and aligned with common carrier LTL rating workflows.

## Special Services / Accessorials
Accessorials are grouped into Pickup Services, Delivery Services, and Shipment Characteristics. They are persisted with the quote and passed into carrier rating/pricing.

## Shipment Confirmation
After a carrier rate is selected and the quote is approved, Valhalla requires the actual ship-from and ship-to facilities. Users can select Customer 360 saved locations or enter a verified address. The final ZIP must match the rated ZIP or Valhalla blocks shipment creation and requires a re-rate.

## Customer-location defaults
When a saved customer facility is selected, Valhalla automatically carries its operational defaults into the shipment:
- Appointment required
- Liftgate
- Residential
- Limited access

These defaults are additive to accessorials selected during quoting and can be reviewed before shipment creation.

## Documents
Approved shipments automatically receive a Valhalla BOL number. Shipment Control Tower provides printable BOL and shipping-label documents. These browser-generated documents are the development foundation for later carrier-specific label/BOL APIs and S3-backed document storage.
