# Valhalla Freight v0.11.3

## Quote markup panel placement

Quote markup has been moved out of the Carrier Options card in Quote Studio.

The right side of Quote Studio is now intentionally stacked as two separate cards:

1. **Carrier Options** — rate results, selection, and customer quote actions only.
2. **Quote Markup** — internal Flat or Margin pricing override directly beneath Carrier Options.

Markup continues to be folded into the customer sell price and is never presented to the customer as a separate line-item charge.
