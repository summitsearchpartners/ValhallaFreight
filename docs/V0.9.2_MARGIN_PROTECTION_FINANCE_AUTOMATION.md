# Valhalla Freight v0.9.2 — Customer Margin Protection & Freight Audit Automation

## Purpose
Margin protection is now a customer-level policy rather than a global hard-coded threshold.

Each customer can define a carrier invoice variance tolerance. Example: a threshold of $100 means carrier invoices with an absolute quoted-vs-final variance of $100 or less remain within tolerance; a variance above $100 becomes a freight-audit exception.

## Workflow
1. Carrier invoice enters Valhalla through a connected carrier adapter or the finance ingestion API.
2. Valhalla matches the invoice to a shipment by Shipment Number, then BOL, then PRO.
3. Quoted carrier cost is compared to the invoice total.
4. The customer's margin-protection threshold is read from Customer 360.
5. If the absolute variance exceeds the threshold, the carrier bill is flagged `exception`.
6. Valhalla creates an internal notification and, when enabled, a customer-portal notification.
7. Operations reviews the charge breakdown and can approve or dispute the carrier bill.
8. Approving the bill writes the invoice total to `final_carrier_cost` for actual GP reporting.

## Customer controls
Customer 360 > Commercial now provides:
- Margin protection threshold ($)
- Notify Valhalla operations
- Notify customer portal

The Customer Portal preview exposes the same threshold control so the eventual customer-scoped login experience can use the exact same API and data model.

## Carrier API architecture
`POST /api/v1/finance/ingest/carrier-invoice` is the normalized invoice-ingestion boundary. Carrier adapters that expose invoice/billing APIs should map the vendor invoice into this endpoint/service rather than writing finance records directly.

Not every carrier exposes invoice APIs. For carriers without one, invoices can later be ingested from EDI 210, accounting integrations, email/document automation, or portal/document retrieval.

## Default
Existing customers default to a $75.00 threshold to preserve the previous Valhalla behavior until their policy is explicitly changed.
