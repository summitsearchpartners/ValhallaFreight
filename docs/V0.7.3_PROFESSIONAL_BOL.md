# Valhalla Freight v0.7.3 - Professional Bill of Lading

The Shipment Control Tower BOL was rebuilt into a carrier-style straight Bill of Lading. The layout is inspired by established LTL BOL conventions, including the Dayton Freight example supplied during development, without copying Dayton branding or exact legal language.

## Auto-populated sections
- Valhalla BOL number and shipment number
- Carrier, SCAC and PRO
- Ship-from and ship-to facility information
- Saved Customer 360 location contact details and instructions
- Customer account
- Freight charge terms
- Special services/accessorials
- Required delivery and scheduled pickup
- Handling unit quantity/type
- Freight class, NMFC, hazmat, commodity description, dimensions and weight
- Shipment totals
- PRO barcode/sticker area
- Shipper and carrier signature areas

The print view is optimized for US Letter and can be printed or saved as PDF from the browser.
