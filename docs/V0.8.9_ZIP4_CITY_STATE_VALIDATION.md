# Valhalla Freight v0.8.9 — ZIP+4 & Verified City/State Validation

Shipment Confirmation now treats a US ZIP+4 (for example `66061-3554`) as the same rated geography as its five-digit ZIP (`66061`). Google-verified addresses preserve the verified city, state, and full postal code. Approval validates the five-digit rated ZIP and also ensures the displayed city/state have not been altered after the Google selection.

This prevents false failures for valid ZIP+4 addresses while still blocking facilities that are actually outside the rated lane. Saved Customer 360 locations continue to be filtered and validated against the rated ZIP.
